# Z Biz Plan

Turn an approved Z-Knowledge Brief into the smallest useful plan for the current decision and work mode.

## Confirm This Is Planning Work

- Use Biz-Plan when the intended result is an executable business, marketing, and action plan.
- Use Brief or Research when the work is still mainly an overview, investigation, comparison, or evidence package.
- Start from an existing Brief when one exists. Search only the approved Z-Knowledge databases for the subject, related Brief, and any suitable existing Biz-Plan.
- Update a suitable existing Biz-Plan instead of creating a duplicate. Keep the Brief and source material unchanged; the Biz-Plan is a linked derivative record.

## Load The Governing Sources

Before drafting or updating a plan, read:

- [Biz-Plan Template](https://app.notion.com/p/3a2a3e33d58181b8ae02cef01f218b86)
- [Biz-Plan Template SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3a2a3e33d581810a8080dc4d30104419)

Use their current versions. The template controls plan structure and the SOP controls the detailed working procedure; do not copy either into this skill.

## Build Only What The Decision Needs

- State the decision or result the plan must support and choose Research, Planning, Implementation, or Review mode.
- Use the related Brief, approved research, sources, decisions, and linked records as inputs.
- Complete only the sections required for the current mode and decision. Go deeper when cost, risk, uncertainty, or reversibility justifies it.
- Distinguish confirmed facts, assumptions, missing research, planned actions, completed work, and measured results using the labels defined by the current template and SOP.
- Keep the plan practical. Identify the immediate decision, next actions, owners, deadlines, success measures, decision thresholds, and material risks.
- Do not invent facts, sources, financial figures, customer evidence, completed work, or results to fill the template.

## Preserve Subject Identity

- The Biz-Plan belongs to the same subject and authoritative Z-Knowledge database as the related Brief unless an approved routing exception applies.
- Carry forward the approved subject identity, Name-Key, and shared six-digit Z-Code from the Brief.
- If the correct Brief, database, identity, or existing Biz-Plan cannot be confirmed, stop before creating a second source of truth and report what must be resolved.

## Hand Off Publishing

Use `z-notion-knowledge-publish` for Notion creation or updates, property values, relations, Z-Code suffix selection, icons, and final verification.

Provide that handoff with:

- the related Brief and target database;
- whether to create or update the Biz-Plan;
- the approved Name-Key and shared six-digit Z-Code;
- the completed plan content and work mode;
- related records and sources to link;
- unresolved assumptions, risks, or routing questions.

Do not treat a drafted plan as published until the publishing handoff verifies the final record.
